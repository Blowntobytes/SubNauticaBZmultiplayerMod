# Changelog

## 0.0.1 - First release
- Co-op for Subnautica: Below Zero over Steam (friends-only lobby, up to 8 players), VR-first via SubmersedVR_BZ, flat-screen welcome.
- Host's world is streamed to joiners automatically (save slot `slot9990`); each joiner's inventory is kept per world by the host.
- Players shown with the game's diver model; VR head and hand tracking; held items and an open PDA shown in hand; markers for players over 50 m away.
- Shared: day/night clock, weather, items, outcrops, storage, building and base pieces (including deconstruction), story goals, PDA log, encyclopedia, blueprints, story cutscenes.
- Shared vehicles: Mobile Vehicle Bay, Snowfox, Seatruck and modules, Prawn suit, with docking and seated passengers; a vehicle being driven can't be taken over.
- Joiners spawn at the nearest base or the drop pod.
- Time keeps running while a player is in the PDA or pause menu.
- Multiplayer menu (main menu and Options), F5/F4/Home/End keys, Steam invites, Discord Join.
- VR: walk/sprint 1.5x on land by default; Auto sprint option.
